I am interested in how neural networks respond to stimulation and how electrophysiology can help us study living biological systems. My research has taken me from olfactory cell cultures to adult zebrafish retina, combining hands-on experimental work with neural activity analysis.

01 / CRTD · TU Dresden · 2024

Establishing HD-MEA Recordings in Adult Zebrafish Retina

My master's thesis focused on establishing ex vivo retinal recordings using 3Brain high-density microelectrode arrays, investigating spontaneous and light-evoked neuronal activity, and analyzing responses to pharmacological and light-lesion conditions.
