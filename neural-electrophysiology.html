01 / From structure to function

Neuronal markers show that cells express aspects of neural identity. Electrophysiology asks whether they generate electrical activity and communicate in networks.

02 / Extracellular recordings

Microelectrode arrays record voltage changes associated with activity near electrodes. Depending on the preparation and analysis, researchers quantify spikes, firing rates, bursts, synchrony and stimulus-related responses.

03 / What an MEA does not measure directly

Extracellular spikes do not directly reveal membrane potential or intracellular currents in the way conventional patch clamp can. Interpretation depends on electrode contact, signal quality and analysis choices.

04 / From recording to stimulation

Electrical interfaces can also deliver stimulation. Closed-loop experiments use recorded responses to guide subsequent inputs, enabling experiments on adaptation and feedback. [1]

05 / My connection

My experimental work includes Axion MEA recordings of olfactory cultures and HD-MEA recordings of adult zebrafish retina. These are my previous projects, not experiments in organoid intelligence. Explore my research experience or HD-MEAs.
