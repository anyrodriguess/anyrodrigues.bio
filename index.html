Hi, I'm Any Rodrigues, a molecular bioengineer interested in the interface between living neural systems and technology.

My background is in neural electrophysiology and HD-MEA, working with neural co-cultures and ex vivo adult zebrafish retina.

I am particularly interested in moving from simply recording neural activity toward bidirectional systems that can stimulate, record and interact with living neural networks.
